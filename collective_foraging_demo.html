Collective foraging from simple recruitment cascades:

a maximum-entropy path to natural, learner-free reinforcement learning

A threshold contagion (Watts/Granovetter LTM) embedded in space, where the signal outruns the agents: a discovery recruits neighbours through a cascade on the proximity graph; fraction-normalised activation both spreads it and stifles it once crowded (blocking); the cascade rewires its own graph. Resources are sparse and clumped — the entropically generic (maximum-entropy) field. One running swarm — and the elegant coincidences build live in the panels: the self-organized cascade window, the poised SOC drive–avalanche cycle, scale-free avalanches, marginal-value patch-leaving, the ideal-free distribution (reinforcement learning with no learner), the two maximum entropies (Lévy search ↔ resource field), and near-critical branching.
